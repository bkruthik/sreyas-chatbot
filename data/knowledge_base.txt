== ADMISSIONS AND ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA ==
1. Category-A Seats (Convenor Quota - 70% of total intake):
- Admission Path: Through TG EAPCET (Telangana Engineering, Agriculture and Pharmacy Common Entrance Test) Counselling conducted by TSCHE.
- Eligibility: Pass in 10+2 / Intermediate examination with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry (MPC) as optional subjects with minimum 45% aggregate marks (40% for reserved categories SC/ST/BC).
- College Code: SREY

2. Category-B Seats (Management / NRI Quota - 30% of total intake):
- Admission Path: Direct Admission based on Merit in 10+2 / Intermediate marks or TG EAPCET / JEE Mains Rank.
- Eligibility: Minimum 45% aggregate marks in PCM at 10+2 level.
- Registration: Applications invited through college portal and official notification.

3. B.Tech Lateral Entry Admission (ECET Quota):
- Admission Path: Direct admission into 2nd Year B.Tech (3rd Semester) through TG ECET Counselling.
- Eligibility: Diploma in Engineering / Technology or B.Sc (Mathematics) degree with minimum 45% marks (40% for reserved categories).

2. R25 Academic Regulations (B.Tech 2025 Onwards Batches):
- Internal Continuous Assessment (Mid Exams, Assignments & Quizzes): 30 Marks per subject.

A: 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM, Monday through Saturday.

Q: What is MSAT at Sreyas?
A: MSAT stands for Management Scholastic Aptitude Test. It is Sreyas Institute's own internal entrance test conducted for students seeking admission under the Management / Category-B quota. Students who appear for MSAT and score well may be eligible for fee concessions and merit seats. MSAT covers topics in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, General Aptitude, and Logical Reasoning. Contact: 9246323444 or info@sreyas.ac.in.

Q: Is MSAT compulsory for admission?
A: MSAT is optional for Management Quota (Category-B) admission. Students with valid TG EAPCET / JEE Mains rank can directly apply. However, students without an entrance rank can appear for MSAT to qualify for merit-based Category-B admission. Students with good 12th Intermediate marks (above 80%) may also be considered without MSAT.

Q: What is the MSAT test pattern?
A: MSAT (Management Scholastic Aptitude Test) consists of approximately 60 to 80 multiple-choice questions. Sections include: Mathematics (25 marks), Physics (20 marks), Chemistry (15 marks), and General Aptitude / Reasoning (20 marks). Duration: 90 minutes. No negative marking. The test is conducted at Sreyas campus. For schedule and registration: 9246323444.

Q: Can I get admission without EAPCET rank?
A: Yes. Under Category-B (Management Quota), students who do not have a TG EAPCET rank can apply directly with 12th Intermediate marks (MPC). Students with 12th marks above 60% are generally eligible. Appearing for MSAT (Sreyas internal test) strengthens your application. Contact admissions desk: 9246323444 for direct application.

Q: What documents are required for admission?
A: Required documents include: (1) 10th / SSC Marks Memo & Certificate, (2) 12th / Intermediate Marks Memo (all subjects), (3) Transfer Certificate (TC) from previous institution, (4) Migration Certificate (from board / university), (5) TG EAPCET Rank Card (if applicable), (6) Aadhar Card (student and parent), (7) Caste Certificate (for SC/ST/BC/OBC), (8) Income Certificate (for scholarship), (9) Passport-size photographs (6 copies), (10) Community Certificate (where applicable).

Q: What is the campus working schedule?
A: College working hours: 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. Administrative office: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. Closed on Sundays and government gazette holidays.

Q: Does Sreyas have a placement guarantee?
A: Sreyas Institute does not offer a formal placement guarantee. However, the Training & Placements Cell has achieved 85%+ placement rates consistently. Students who complete the CRT (Campus Recruitment Training) program and maintain good academic performance have a significantly higher placement success rate. Multiple companies visit campus every year for on-campus hiring.
